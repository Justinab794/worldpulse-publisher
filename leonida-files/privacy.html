Privacy Policy — Leonida Files

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Leonida Files is an independently operated media project. The Leonida Files publishing application is a private tool used by its owner to prepare and manage the owner's videos and connected YouTube channel. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide public user registration.

1. Google services and account data accessed

When the owner connects a Google account and grants the requested OAuth permissions, the application uses Google services for the following limited purposes:

  • YouTube Data API: to identify the connected channel, upload the owner's videos, set video metadata and thumbnails, check processing and publishing status, and schedule eligible videos when the required publishing checks pass.
  • YouTube Analytics API: to retrieve channel and video performance information, such as views, watch time and engagement, to help the owner evaluate content performance.
  • Google Drive API: to create and manage backup files made by this application in the owner's Drive. Backups may include finished video files, thumbnails, publishing metadata, source manifests and quality-control or rights reports. The application is designed to use the drive.file scope, which limits its Drive access to files created or opened by the application.
  • Gmail API: to send video-ready, upload-status and operational notification emails to the email address configured by the owner. The application uses send-only access and does not read the owner's inbox or email messages.

The application does not ask for or store a Google account password. Google OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer, outside the source-code repository, with restricted operating-system file permissions.

2. Other information processed

The application processes video scripts, narration audio, rendered videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags, source references, quality-control results, rights records, job identifiers and upload status. It may send operational status messages and private backup links through a Telegram bot to the owner's configured Telegram chat.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to operate the owner's Leonida Files production and publishing workflow: preparing and rendering videos, preserving production packages, managing eligible YouTube uploads, scheduling publication, sending the owner's notifications and reviewing channel performance.

4. Storage, sharing and security

Production files and records are stored on the owner's computer. When configured, finished packages and related delivery files are copied to the owner's Google Drive. Drive backups are intended to remain private; the application does not intentionally enable public or anonymous file sharing. OAuth credentials and application secrets are kept outside the source-code repository with restricted file permissions.

Notification emails are sent through the owner's Gmail account to the address configured by the owner. Telegram notifications are sent to the owner's configured chat. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or make it available to unrelated third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local production files and records, Google Drive backups, sent email notifications and Telegram messages may remain available until the owner deletes them. The owner controls the local project files and Drive backups and can delete them. The owner can also revoke the application's Google access through Google Account permissions and can disable or remove the Telegram bot integration.

6. Third-party services

The application uses services operated by Google, including YouTube, Google Drive and Gmail, and may use Telegram to deliver owner notifications. Their services are subject to their own terms and privacy practices. See the YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy and Telegram Privacy Policy.

7. Revoking Google access

The owner can review or revoke the application's Google account permissions at Google Account permissions. Revoking access prevents further authorized Google API requests until access is granted again.

8. Children's privacy

The application is a private owner-operated publishing tool and is not designed for children or for collecting personal information from viewers.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data-handling practices change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.
