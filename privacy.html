Privacy Policy — WorldPulse Publisher

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

WorldPulse Publisher is a privately operated application used by the owner of the WorldPulse YouTube channel to upload videos, schedule publication, verify publishing status and review channel analytics. It is not offered as a public service.

2. Information accessed

With the channel owner's authorization, the application accesses YouTube channel identity and information, video metadata and publishing status, and channel analytics such as views, watch time, engagement and audience information. It requests YouTube permissions for channel/video management and read-only analytics.

The application does not request Gmail or Google Drive access, and it does not ask for or store the user's Google password.

3. How information is used

Information is used solely to upload and manage the owner's WorldPulse videos, schedule publication, verify uploads, and review channel performance to inform future publishing decisions.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens, application configuration and publishing records are intended to be stored locally on the channel owner's computer, outside the source-code repository, with operating-system file permissions restricting access. The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Local publishing records and authorization tokens are retained until removed by the application owner. The owner can delete local application data and revoke Google access at any time.

6. Third-party services

The application uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, operated by Google. Use of YouTube services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

7. Revoking access

Google account permissions can be reviewed or revoked at Google account permissions. Revoking access stops further authorized API requests until access is granted again.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or the application, use the developer contact email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.

9. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.
